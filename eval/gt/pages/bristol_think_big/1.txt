Scholarships for international students
International students can apply for a range of funding, including Think Big scholarships.
Think Big is the University of Bristol’s flagship scholarship scheme. You can also search for other scholarships for international students.
Think Big international scholarships
Our Think Big international scholarships for undergraduate and postgraduate students could help you fund your UK study at the University of Bristol. We've invested up to £5 million in Think Big scholarships to help international students come to Bristol in 2027.
These scholarships are for applicants with overseas fee status who have applied for an eligible undergraduate or master's course.
Scholarships available
Think Big undergraduate scholarships are valued at £3,000, £6,500 and £13,000 for up to four years of study.
Think Big postgraduate scholarships are valued at £6,500, £13,000 and £26,000 for your first year of study.
Multiple scholarships, one application form
Our Think Big Scholarship scheme has partnered with faculties, alumni and external partners to offer a wide range of scholarships in addition to the central Think Big undergraduate and postgraduate awards.
To make the application process easier, You only need to fill in one application form to be considered for the international scholarships that you are eligible for.
