# UCL Global Master's Scholarship

The UCL Global Master's Scholarship aims to enable and encourage international students from lower income backgrounds to pursue full-time Master's degree studies at UCL.

YouTube Widget Placeholder<https://youtu.be/Q-c5XhcbQN4>

## Key information

**Value:** £15,000 (for one year)  
**Available to:** Prospective students  
**Selection criteria:** Financial need  
**Eligible fee status:** Overseas  
**Eligible programmes:** All postgraduate taught  
**Deadline:** 5pm BST, Thursday 7 May 2026

## How to apply

Please visit the [International Master's Awards](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/scholarships/international-masters-awards) page for information on how to apply.

The deadline for applications to this scholarship will be at 5pm BST, Thursday 7 May 2026.

## Results

Shortlisted candidates will be contacted mid May to provide evidence of financial need. When this has been assessed, successful candidates will typically be informed by late June.
