Quick Answer

**The Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals (SISGP) are the Swedish government’s flagship master’s scholarship** — fully funding tuition, a monthly living stipend of SEK 12,000, a travel grant, and insurance for one- or two-year master’s programmes at Swedish universities.

It is open only to citizens of **34 eligible countries** who have at least **3,000 hours of work experience** (a lower threshold applies to six Eastern European countries) and documented leadership experience. In the most recently completed cycle, **189 SISGP scholarships** were awarded from **12,922 applications** — an acceptance rate of roughly **1.5%**; the rate has run **4–5%** in prior cycles.

The next application cycle is expected to follow the usual pattern, with a university deadline around **15 January 2027** and an SI scholarship window in **February 2027** — the Swedish Institute typically confirms exact 2027/28 dates in mid-November 2026.

The SI Scholarship for Global Professionals is one of the most generous fully funded master’s scholarships in Europe — and one of the most competitive. Funded by the Swedish government through the Swedish Institute, it covers full tuition, a monthly living stipend, travel, and insurance for master’s study at any Swedish university. In the most recently completed cycle, 189 SISGP scholarships (232 including the linked Pioneering Women in STEM award) were selected from across 34 eligible countries.

But the SI Scholarship is not like Clarendon or Knight-Hennessy. It has a **work experience requirement**, a **leadership requirement**, and a strictly limited **eligible country list**. It also requires **two separate applications** on two different deadlines — one to a Swedish university and one to the Swedish Institute. Missing either disqualifies you entirely.

## **What Is the SI Scholarship for Global Professionals?**

The SI Scholarship for Global Professionals is the Swedish government’s flagship scholarship for international master’s students. It is administered by the Swedish Institute (Svenska institutet), a public agency responsible for Sweden’s international cultural and educational exchange.

The programme is designed for professionals — not fresh graduates — who have demonstrated leadership in their careers or communities and who intend to return home to contribute to sustainable development. It is explicitly linked to the United Nations 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development.

Here is what makes the SI Scholarship unique:

* **A work experience requirement.** Unlike most fully funded scholarships, SI requires documented professional experience — a minimum of 3,000 hours for most countries, with a reduced threshold for six Eastern European countries.
* **A leadership requirement.** Applicants must demonstrate leadership from their employer or civil society engagement.
* **A limited eligible country list.** Only 34 countries qualify. This is not a global scholarship.
* **A development mission.** SI selects scholars who plan to return home and contribute to sustainable development.
* **Two separate applications.** You must apply to a Swedish master’s programme AND the SI Scholarship separately, with two different deadlines.
* **No age limit.** Unlike Rhodes, SI does not restrict by age.

**💡 LearnViaHub Tip:** The SI Scholarship is not for everyone. If your country is not on the eligible list, or if you do not have the required work and leadership experience, consider the [Clarendon Fund](https://www.learnviahub.com/clarendon-fund-scholarships) at Oxford — it has no nationality restrictions and no work experience requirement.

## **SI Scholarship Eligibility Requirements**

The SI Scholarship has some of the most specific eligibility rules of any major scholarship. Here is the summary — for the full checklist, read our dedicated guide.

### ✅ Core Requirements

* **Citizenship:** One of 34 eligible countries (Africa, Asia, Eastern Europe, Latin America).
* **Work experience:** At least 3,000 hours (roughly 18 months full-time). Internships do not count. Applicants from Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Georgia, Moldova, and Ukraine are held to a lower threshold — any documented professional experience counts toward this requirement.
* **Leadership experience:** Documented from employer or civil society engagement.
* **University application:** Must apply to an eligible master’s programme by the university deadline (expected mid-January 2027).
* **Admission:** Must be admitted (or conditionally admitted) to an eligible programme.
* **No prior SI scholarship:** Previous SI recipients are not eligible.
* **No Swedish degree:** Applicants who hold a Swedish degree or are currently enrolled are not eligible.
* **Sweden residency limit:** Applicants who have lived in Sweden for 2+ cumulative years are not eligible.

**❌ Not Eligible:** Applicants from countries not on the 34-country list, EU/EEA citizens, applicants with only internship experience, previous SI Scholarship recipients, current or former Swedish university students, and applicants who have lived in Sweden for two or more cumulative years.

**Need the full checklist?** Read our detailed [SI Scholarship Eligibility Requirements](https://www.learnviahub.com/si-scholarship-eligibility/) guide.

## **What Does the SI Scholarship Cover?**

The SI Scholarship is fully funded and covers all major costs of studying a master’s in Sweden.

| Coverage Area | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Tuition Fees** | Full coverage of tuition fees for the master’s programme, paid directly to your university |
| **Living Stipend** | SEK 12,000 per month (approximately £900 / $1,150) |
| **Travel Grant** | One-time payment of up to SEK 15,000 for travel to and from Sweden — reduced to SEK 10,000 for scholars from Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Georgia, Moldova, and Ukraine. Not paid to applicants already living in Sweden. |
| **Insurance** | Health insurance coverage during the scholarship period |
| **Alumni Network** | Access to the SI Network for Global Professionals (NFGP) and the wider SI Alumni Network |

**What It Does NOT Cover:** Family/dependant costs, personal travel beyond the one-time travel grant, and expenses beyond the monthly stipend. The scholarship does not pay for the university application fee, which you must cover yourself.

**💡 LearnViaHub Tip:** The SEK 12,000 monthly stipend is designed to cover living costs in Sweden. Rent is the largest expense — in Stockholm, a student room can cost SEK 5,000–8,000. Plan your budget carefully before accepting the scholarship.

## **How to Apply for the SI Scholarship**

The SI Scholarship application process has **two separate steps** that must be completed in order. Failing either one disqualifies you from the scholarship.

**You must submit two separate applications on two different deadlines:** (1) a university master’s programme application through universityadmissions.se by the university deadline (expected mid-January 2027), and (2) an SI Scholarship application through the SI portal during its ~14-day window (expected late February 2027). Both are required.

### The Application Process (Simplified)

1. **Confirm your eligibility.** Check the 34-country list, work experience rule, and leadership requirement.
2. **Apply to an eligible master’s programme.** Submit your university application via universityadmissions.se by the university deadline and pay the application fee. The list of eligible master’s programmes is typically published by SI in mid-November.
3. **Prepare your work and leadership forms.** Use the current SI templates — previous years’ templates are rejected.
4. **Wait for the scholarship portal to open.** The SI scholarship portal typically opens in early-to-mid February.
5. **Submit your scholarship application.** Complete it within the ~14-day window before the portal closes (14:59 CET on the closing date).
6. **Wait for admission results.** University admission results are typically published in late March.
7. **Wait for scholarship results.** Recipients are typically announced in late April.
8. **Accept your offer.** If selected, accept by the deadline in your offer e-mail (typically late May).

**⚠️ Critical:** Many applicants focus on the February scholarship deadline and miss the January university deadline entirely. Both are mandatory. There is no workaround for missing either.

### What Makes a Competitive Candidate

* **Documented leadership experience** — the most differentiating factor in the selection process.
* **A clear development narrative** — a specific plan for contributing to sustainable development in your home country.
* **Strong academic record** — competitive for your chosen master’s programme.
* **Well-written motivation letter** — connecting your past, present, and future in a coherent arc.

## **SI Scholarship Selection Criteria**

The SI Scholarship selection process evaluates applicants against three criteria. These are assessed throughout your application — motivation letter, work and leadership forms, and academic credentials.

### 1. Leadership Potential

The most differentiating factor in the selection process. SI looks for evidence that you have initiated, built, or changed something — not just held titles. Concrete examples of leading teams, projects, or civil society initiatives carry the most weight. Your leadership narrative is the single highest-leverage part of your application.

### 2. Academic Excellence

A baseline requirement. You must demonstrate strong academic performance and be competitive for your chosen master’s programme. Grades are often similar across finalists, so this is not a differentiator at the final stage — but a weak academic record can eliminate you early.

### 3. Capacity to Contribute to Sustainable Development

SI looks for a clear, specific, and credible plan for post-study impact. Your motivation letter should articulate why this specific programme, at this specific moment, fits into a larger trajectory of development work in your home country. Generic statements about “changing the world” will not survive the first screen.

**💡 LearnViaHub Tip:** Before you write your motivation letter, ask yourself: “If I stripped away my grades and work experience, what evidence would remain that I have led real change?” If you cannot answer clearly, your application is not ready.

## ❓ **SI Scholarship FAQs**

What is the SI Scholarship for Global Professionals?

The SI Scholarship for Global Professionals is the Swedish government’s flagship master’s scholarship, funded by the Swedish Institute. It covers full tuition, a monthly living stipend of SEK 12,000, travel, and insurance for master’s study at any Swedish university.

  How many SI Scholarships are awarded each year?

In the most recently completed cycle, 189 SISGP scholarships were awarded, plus 43 through the linked Pioneering Women in STEM scholarship — 232 combined. In the prior cycle, 351 SISGP scholarships were awarded (402 combined with Pioneering Women in STEM). The number fluctuates year to year based on the Swedish Institute’s budget and applicant volume.

  What does the SI Scholarship cover?

The SI Scholarship covers full tuition fees, a monthly living stipend of SEK 12,000, a one-time travel grant (up to SEK 15,000, or SEK 10,000 for scholars from Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Georgia, Moldova, and Ukraine), and health insurance. It does not cover dependants, personal expenses beyond the stipend, or the university application fee.

  Which countries are eligible for the SI Scholarship?

34 countries across Africa, Asia, Eastern Europe, and Latin America. The full list is available on si.se and includes Egypt, Ethiopia, Kenya, Liberia, Morocco, Nigeria, Rwanda, Senegal, South Africa, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Iraq, Jordan, Malaysia, Philippines, Thailand, Uzbekistan, Vietnam, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Georgia, Moldova, Ukraine, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Guatemala, Mexico, and Peru. Always confirm against the current si.se list, as it is reviewed annually.

  Do internships count toward the 3,000-hour work experience requirement?

No. Internships are explicitly excluded from the work experience requirement, regardless of duration or whether they were paid. Only full-time employment, part-time employment, freelancing, and self-employment count. Applicants from Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Georgia, Moldova, and Ukraine are held to a lower threshold and do not need to meet the 3,000-hour minimum.

  Is there an age limit for the SI Scholarship?

No. Unlike the Rhodes Scholarship, the SI Scholarship has no age limit. Applicants in their 30s and 40s are regularly accepted.

  What is the acceptance rate for the SI Scholarship?

In the most recently completed cycle, the acceptance rate was approximately 1.5% (189 SISGP awards from 12,922 applications). In the prior cycle, the rate was closer to 4% (351 awards from around 8,580 applications). The rate has fallen sharply due to a large increase in applications alongside a reduction in awards.

  Can I apply for the SI Scholarship and other scholarships at the same time?

Yes. The SI Scholarship does not prohibit applying for other scholarships. If you are also applying to Oxford or Stanford, consider the [Clarendon Fund](https://www.learnviahub.com/clarendon-fund-scholarships), [Rhodes Scholarship](https://www.learnviahub.com/rhodes-scholarship/), and [Knight-Hennessy Scholars](https://www.learnviahub.com/knight-hennessy-scholars/).
