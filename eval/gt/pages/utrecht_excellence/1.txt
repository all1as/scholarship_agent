Utrecht Excellence Scholarships
Due to significant budget cuts, the Utrecht Excellence Scholarship (UES) will no longer be offered for programmes starting in the 2026–2027 academic year.
No new UES applications will be accepted as of the upcoming admissions cycle.
If you have already been awarded a UES, your scholarship remains unchanged and will continue as agreed.
We understand this may be disappointing and appreciate your understanding as we work toward long-term sustainability.
