# Uppsala University Scholarship 2027 | Step-by-Step Process

Updated:

If you are planning to study in Sweden with financial support, the ****Uppsala University Scholarship**** is one of the best opportunities available for international students. This scholarship provides [fully funded](https://scholarshiproar.com/fully-funded-scholarships/) programs across a wide range of fields. In this article, we have explained everything you need to know about the Uppsala University Scholarship, including its benefits, eligibility criteria, required documents, and a detailed step-by-step application process to help you apply successfully.

**The Uppsala University Scholarship 2027-2028 is available for [master’s](https://scholarshiproar.com/masters-scholarships/) studies in any subject and covers the full cost of tuition fees for studies at Uppsala University.**

Uppsala University Global Scholarships are open for international students to pursue their master’s degrees in Sweden. Several scholarships are available for fee-paying international students who apply to an international bachelor’s or master’s program each year.

Uppsala University is recognized internationally as one of the world’s top 100 universities. UU has been providing technical and pedagogical education for over 500 years, implementing the highest academic standards and conducting research that really makes a difference. We have successfully recruited some of the world’s best teaching staff, and our goal is to educate and inspire.

## The Uppsala University Scholarship Application Deadline

The deadline for the Uppsala University Scholarship is January 16, 2027 (admission), 16 January 2027, to 2 February 2027 (scholarship).

## Scholarship Coverage

The Uppsala University Scholarship will cover the full cost of tuition fees for studies at Uppsala University.

## Eligibility Criteria for Uppsala University Scholarship

To qualify for the Uppsala University Scholarship, a candidate must fulfill all of the following requirements:

* **Required Language:**English
* **Eligible Countries:** Citizens of a country outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland.
* Applicants must demonstrate academic talent and show interest in belonging to an educational milieu.
* Students can only be awarded an IPK scholarship if a program at Uppsala University is chosen as a priority.
* You must meet the entrance requirements for the program you applied to, and the application fee and supporting documents must have been received before the deadline by the university admissions.
* The program must be delivered on campus. The scholarship is not available for distance learning programs or programs partially taught at other universities outside Sweden.

## How to Apply for the Uppsala University Scholarship?

Please follow the instructions below to apply for this scholarship:

1. You must first submit an application for a bachelor’s or master’s program at Uppsala University on [universityadmissions. See](https://www.universityadmissions.se/intl/start) between 18 October 2026 and 10 January 2027. The program at Uppsala University must be [ranked](https://www.universityadmissions.se/en/apply-to-masters/rank-your-selections-masters/) as your first priority of choice. The scholarship application period starts after this step.
2. To apply for our scholarships, you need to submit an online application form during our scholarship application period. Access to the application form will be available on this page during the scholarship application period, **10–22 January 2027.**

**Note:**

* We will also review the supporting documents that you submit for your program application at [universityadmissions.se](https://www.universityadmissions.se/intl/start). Therefore, you do not need to submit any extra documents in your scholarship application form.
* Read the detailed information about [the scholarship application process](https://www.uu.se/en/study/masters-studies/scholarships/application-guide).
