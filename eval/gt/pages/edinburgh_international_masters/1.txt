# Funding for international students

In addition to financial assistance provided by UK Government agencies and external funding sources, the University offers a number of general and subject-specific funding schemes.

## [Awards by region](/student-funding/postgraduate/international/region)

These pages provide specific information on international postgraduate scholarships for prospective students.

## [College of Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences](/student-funding/postgraduate/international/humanities)

Cross-disciplinary awards as well as many subject-specific scholarships are available.

## [College of Medicine and Veterinary Medicine](/student-funding/postgraduate/international/medicine-vet-medicine)

International students can compete in a variety of funding schemes.

## [College of Science and Engineering](/student-funding/postgraduate/international/science-engineering)

The College operates numerous scholarship schemes funded through University funds, organisations and industrial sponsors.

## [Other Funding](/student-funding/postgraduate/international/other-funding)

Information of external funding opportunities available to prospective postgraduate international students.

## [Edinburgh Doctoral College Scholarships](/student-funding/postgraduate/international/doctoral-college)

The University will offer PhD scholarships for students starting their PhD research at the University in the 2027-2028 academic session.

## [GREAT Scholarships 2026](/scholarships-and-student-funding/prospective-postgraduates/funding-for-international-students/great)

In partnership with the British Council and the GREAT Britain Campaign, The University of Edinburgh is offering 3 scholarships to students in Thailand, Mexico and Ghana, applying for one-year postgraduate courses.

## [Arts and Humanities Research Council - Doctoral Landscape Awards](/scholarships-and-student-funding/prospective-postgraduates/funding-for-uk-students/research)

The University of Edinburgh is one of 50 leading Higher Education Institutions to receive a Doctoral Landscape Award from the AHRC to help underpin the strength and stability of the arts and humanities research environment.

## [Economic and Social Research Council](/student-funding/postgraduate/uk-eu/research-councils/esrc)

Funding available from The Scottish Graduate School of Social Science (SGSSS).
