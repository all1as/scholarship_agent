This is an overview of the important dates throughout the application process and the start of your Master's studies. All deadlines are at midnight CET (Central European Time) on the date indicated.
Please note that the dates below may be subject to change.
Submit your application
16 October 2026 - 15 January 2027
The application period for Master's programmes with English as language of instruction is open for national and international students at universityadmissions.se or antagning.se (application deadline at midnight CET).
Take note that the application period for the Master's programme Lärande och ledarskap (with Swedish as the language of instruction) is 15 March and 15 April 2027.
Take note that the October-January application period is not applicable to third year students currently enrolled at a Bachelor's programme at Chalmers, or alumni with a Bachelor's degree from Chalmers, since the application period in April applies to this category.
How to apply
Applying to Chalmers is easy. Find the programme or course you're interested in, check that you meet the entry requirements, and submit your application. Here you will find information about application periods and how to apply.
Apply for a scholarship
Late November 2026 - Mid January 2027
An application for scholarships administered by Chalmers is a separate application process. Take note, the Swedish Institute (SI) scholarships for Global Professionals are not administered by Chalmers.
Scholarships for fee-paying students
Scholarships are a great source of funding for prospective Master's students who are liable to pay tuition fees. Some of these are administered by Chalmers and some by other foundations and associations. Additional scholarships may be added to the list and you are therefore advised to check this webpage regularly.
Deadline for paying the application fee or prove fee-exempt status
1 February 2027
The deadline for paying the application fee or to document your citizenship of an EU/EEA country. If required, pay the application fee to universityadmissions.se. This is a non-refundable administrative fee. Note that one or the other is required in order for your application to be processed.
Find out if you need to pay fees
Wondering if you need to pay application and tuition fees? Here, you will find questions and answers.
Deadline for supporting documentation, to complete your application
1 February 2027
The list of required documents is specific to Chalmers and applies to all applicants, including those with a Swedish personal identity number and/or Swedish citizenship. You can find this list, along with all necessary instructions, at Apply for master's programmes.
Please note that for programmes requiring a portfolio, the submission deadline is 15 January 2027.
Publication of the admission decision
1 April 2027
The admission decision from University Admissions is also available online as a printable PDF file named "Notification of Selection Results" and no hard copy is sent by regular mail. The PDF serves as the official document and can be used if requested. Students who are required to pay tuition fees will have the notification 'Conditionally admitted' on their admission decision. This status remains both at the account and on the PDF even after payment. You can provide a receipt provided by the Tuition fee office for the fee paid if you need to provide proof of this.

Application period for the Master's programme Lärande och ledarskap
15 March to 15 April 2027
Submit your application at antagning.se. Take note that Swedish proficiency is required for this programme Lärande och ledarskap, Msc
Scholarship award notifications
Mid-March to late April 2027
The Scholarship Award Notifications will be sent out (by e-mail) to all grant recipients. In order to keep your scholarship offer, you have to confirm that you accept the offered scholarship. Instructions will follow in your scholarship offer notification. Make sure to confirm by the specified deadline.
All applicants will receive an email with information regardless of whether or not they have been awarded a scholarship. This applies to scholarships administered by Chalmers University of Technology only (the SI scholarships are NOT administered by Chalmers).
Application period in April for Chalmers Bachelor's students only
1 April to 15 April 2027
Third year students currently enrolled at a Bachelor's programme at Chalmers or alumni with a Bachelor's degree from Chalmers have a separate admissions process. Choose your Master's degree programme
Chalmers Pre-Departure Days
April 2027
The week after the admission decision, we will host a digital pre-departure event for all admitted Master's students where you will get to know Chalmers better. We will focus on different relevant topics, to help you prepare for your studies. An email with information will be sent after the admissions decision has been announced, exclusively to students admitted to Chalmers (we will send it to the email address you used when you applied on universityadmissions.se).
The deadline for paying the first semester's tuition fees (for fee-paying students)
1 May 2027
These must have been received by Chalmers by this date (payment for the first out of four semesters). Payment instructions will be sent by email after the admission decision is published. Applicants who are admitted in May (from the waiting list or late applications) will be given an alternative deadline with the payment instructions.
Please pay the tuition fee as soon as possible to avoid missing the deadline. The residence permit application process can take a long time, and you cannot start the application process until you have paid the tuition fees.
