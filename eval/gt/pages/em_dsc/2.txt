DEAI is a highly competitive Erasmus Mundus Joint Master programme attracting talented applicants from around the world. A successful application requires not only a strong academic background but also clear and convincing evidence of your achievements, skills, and potential.
To help you prepare a competitive application, we strongly encourage you to review the following information before applying:
Expected Profiles & Pre-requisites
Discover the recommended academic backgrounds and the technical competences expected from DEAI applicants. Check whether your previous studies provide the necessary foundations to successfully follow the programme.
Hints & Tips
Learn how to strengthen your application by providing relevant evidence of your academic achievements, research experience, motivation, and potential. Find practical recommendations on how to distinguish yourself from other candidates and avoid common mistakes.
Remember: The Selection Committee can only assess the information and evidence you provide. Make sure your application reflects your strengths and achievements!
