### IMLEX Erasmus Mundus (Master of Science in Imaging and Light in Extended Reality)

The IMLEX Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Imaging and Light in Extended Reality is a multidisciplinary international programme that equips graduates with advanced theoretical and practical expertise in imaging, lighting, extended reality (XR) and related digital technologies. Launched as part of the Erasmus+ collaboration between European and Japanese universities, IMLEX combines rigorous academic training with mobility across three European countries and Japan, enabling students to benefit from world-class research, industry exposure and cultural diversity.

Drawing on the strengths of cutting-edge partner institutions, the IMLEX Erasmus Mundus curriculum fosters innovation in computational imaging, XR technologies, rendering and lighting design. Graduates earn a joint Master of Science degree and a national Master of Engineering degree upon successful completion.

| --- | --- |
| **Programme Name** | Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in *Imaging and Light in Extended Reality* (IMLEX) |
| **Duration** | 2 academic years (120 ECTS) |

### Programme Structure

| Year | Location | Academic Components |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Year 1** | Finland (University of Eastern Finland) | Fundamental courses in Photonics, Imaging, Information Technology, Robotics |
| **Year 1 (cont’d)** | France/Belgium (Université Jean Monnet / KU Leuven) | Computational Imaging & Vision or Lighting specialisation; summer school in Lighting |
| **Year 2 (Sem 3)** | Japan (Toyohashi University of Technology) | Advanced topics in Computational Imaging, Lighting and XR |
| **Year 2 (Sem 4)** | Partner university or industry | Master’s thesis project |

### Research and Industry Links

The IMLEX Erasmus Mundus programme collaborates with associate academic partners such as Utsunomiya University, Chiba University and industrial partners in the virtual reality, imaging and lighting sectors whose contributions enrich research and practical training opportunities. Students benefit from engagement with both academic and industry stakeholders across Europe and Japan.

### Admission Requirements

Applicants to the IMLEX Erasmus Mundus must meet the following criteria:

* Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Physics, Mathematics, Electrical Engineering, Photonics or related field with strong academic performance
* Proficiency in English (TOEFL iBT 92, IELTS Academic 6.5, PTE 62 or equivalent)
* Degree equivalent to at least 180 ECTS credits
* Demonstrated background in relevant technical coursework and skills

### Required Documents

Applicants must upload:

* Bachelor’s degree certificate and official transcripts (with English translations where applicable)
* English language certificate
* Curriculum Vitae
* Two recommendation letters
* Motivation letter addressing IMLEX curriculum and goals
* Copy of passport/ID
* (Optional) Motivation video

### How to Apply – Step-By-Step

1. Visit the Studyinfo portal when the application opens (usually December to January)
2. Complete the online application form and upload all documents in PDF format
3. Submit referee contact details for recommendation letters
4. Submit the application before the deadline
5. Conditional admission requires certified degree and transcript submissions later
6. Await admission notification (usually by April)

### Alumni Experience

Graduates of the IMLEX Erasmus Mundus programme often highlight its unique mobility pathway and exposure to international research environments, with many pursuing careers in XR technology development, lighting and imaging innovation, and related advanced research fields. The joint degree and cross-continental experience significantly enhance employability in global high-tech sectors.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

###### **What is the duration of the IMLEX Erasmus Mundus program?**

The program spans two years, divided into four semesters.

###### **Are scholarships available for the IMLEX Erasmus Mundus program?**

Yes, Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degree scholarships are available on a competitive basis.

###### **What degrees will I receive upon completion of the IMLEX Erasmus Mundus program?**

Graduates receive joint degrees from the participating universities.

###### **Is knowledge of Japanese required for the IMLEX Erasmus Mundus program?**

No, the program is conducted in English. However, students may choose to study Japanese language and culture.

###### **Can I apply for the IMLEX Erasmus Mundus program if I have not yet graduated?**

Yes, applicants in the final year of their bachelor’s degree can apply, provided they graduate before the program starts.
