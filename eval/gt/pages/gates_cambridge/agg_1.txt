# Gates Cambridge Scholarship 2027 in UK (Fully Funded)

By

[Muhammad Saim Rasheed](https://opportunitiescorners.com/author/saimusa2017/)

-

The **Gates Cambridge Scholarship 2027** offers a **Fully Funded** **Master’s** and **Ph.D**. Scholarship for students from outside the UK at the **University of Cambridge**. This program was established in 2000 as a Donation from Bill and Melinda Gates to the University of Cambridge, UK. Each year, the Gates Cambridge program selects **80 students** from all around the world to pursue a postgraduate degree in **any** subject available at the University of Cambridge, UK. The Trust has awarded over 2,000 scholarships from more than 100 countries. Below are additional details about the application process, requirements, and benefits.

## **Details About Gates Cambridge Scholarship 2027**

* **Host Country**: UK
* **Scholarship Name**: Gates Cambridge Scholarship
* **University Name**: Cambridge University
* **Scholarship for**: Master, PhD, MSC, MLitt
* **Number of Scholarships**: 80
* **Deadline:** 
  + **(14th October 2026 for USA Residents)**
  + **For all other Nationals (8th Dec 2026 or 6th January 2027)**

### **Benefits of the Gates Scholarship**

The Gates Cambridge Scholarship for international students covers the full cost of studying at Cambridge. This includes:

* University Fee.
* Maintenance Allowance
* Airfare Ticket (Economy Class)
* Visa costs & the cost of the Immigration Health Surcharge.

**The Trust also provides Funding for:**

* Attending conferences and courses.
* Dependent Children Allowance
* Fieldwork Allowance
* Maternity/Paternity funding
* Hardship funding – for unforeseen difficulties facing the scholar.

### **Academic Fields**

The Gates Cambridge scholarship is available in **all academic disciplines,** except:

* Any Undergraduate degree, such as a BA (undergraduate) or a BA affiliated (a second BA)
* Business Doctorate (BusD)
* Master of Business (MBA)
* Master of Finance (MFin)
* MASt courses
* PGCE
* MBBChir Clinical Studies
* MD Doctor of Medicine degree (6 years, part-time, Home students only)
* Graduate Course in Medicine (A101)
* Part-time degrees other than the PhD
* Non-degree courses

### **Eligibility Criteria**

* Applicants from outside the UK are eligible to apply for the Gates Cambridge Scholarship.
* UK Nationals are not eligible.
* Applying to pursue one of the following courses at the University of Cambridge:
  + PhD (full-time or part-time\*)
  + MLitt (full-time)
  + One-year postgraduate course (full-time), with some exceptions
* Applicants with excellent academic skills and grades.
* Fill out the combined application form (Admission + Gates Funding).

**Note**: Current students at Cambridge University can also apply for the Gates Cambridge Scholarship if they wish to apply for the new postgraduate course. For example, if you are currently enrolled in an MPhil, you can apply for a Gates Cambridge Scholarship to pursue a PhD.

### **How to Apply for the Gates Cambridge Scholarship 2027?**

Follow the application steps:

* **A Combined Application Form:** Applicants submit their application for admission and funding (Gates Cambridge and other funding) via the University’s Graduate Application Portal.
* To be considered for the Gates Cambridge scholarship, you must complete all the sections to apply for admission to a course.
* Go to the **[Course Directory](https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/courses)** and find the course you wish to apply for.
* **Admission Section**: All international applicants, please apply by the Course deadline referenced in the **[University Course Directory](https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/courses)**.
* **The Funding Section**: In this section, you can apply for the Gates Cambridge Scholarship and other funding.
* Gates Cambridge has access to the full application for admission and funding when reviewing applications.

### **Gates Cambridge Scholarship Requirements**

* Apply for admission online at Cambridge University **(see [Course Directory](https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/courses))** and fill out the Gates scholarship section for funding.
* Upload Gates Cambridge statement (approximately 500 words)
* Research proposal (PhD applicants only)
* Gates Cambridge reference (two academic references)

Watch the Cambridge University video on the Gates Application Process (**[here](https://www.gatescambridge.org/apply/how-to-apply/)**). The Link to the official website is given below.
