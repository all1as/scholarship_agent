Full timeline
| Date | Milestone | Applies to | Details | 
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 December | Application portal opens | All applicants | The online portal opens for all programmes – both the regular 2-year MSc and the Industry Master of Science in Engineering. | 
| 15 January | Application deadline | Non-EU | Submit completed application Pay the application fee Submit and fulfil language test requirements Apply for readmission (if previously enrolled at DTU) Apply for exemption (dispensation) | 
| 1 March | Application deadline | EU/EEA | Submit completed application Submit and fulfil language test requirements Apply for readmission (if previously enrolled at DTU) Apply for exemption (dispensation) | 
| April | Tuition fee waivers announced | Non-EU | Tuition fee waivers are awarded to eligible non-EU/EEA applicants. | 
| 1 May | Tuition fee payment deadline | Non-EU | Deadline to pay the tuition fee for non-EU/EEA students. | 
| 15 July | Final document deadline | Non-EU | Submit your final diploma, diploma supplement, or completed academic transcripts. | 
| 15 August | Final document deadline | EU/EEA | Submit your final diploma, diploma supplement, or completed academic transcripts. | 
| Week before semester start | Introduction week | All students | Welcome week for all new MSc students. | 
| 1 September | Semester starts | All students | Autumn semester begins. | 
Degree acquired in Denmark? There is no application fee if your qualifying degree was obtained in Denmark.
Applying for an MSc at DTU?
We strongly recommend applying as early as possible, especially if there are matters requiring further documentation or exemption. It is also recommended that you provide all the relevant documents at the time of submission of your application.
