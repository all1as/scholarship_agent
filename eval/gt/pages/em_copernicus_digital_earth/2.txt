Young Graduates
The Master of Science programme is designed to help recent graduates and early-career professionals strengthen their careers in Earth Observation (EO) & Geographic Information (GI).
INTERNATIONAL APPLICANTS
Please be prepared to submit certified documents with your application!
Your original educational qualifications may either require “full diplomatic accreditation”, a verification by means of a “Hague Apostille”, etc. Please review information: choose Master’s degree, choose Citizenship, choose Nationality of school leaving certificate for university entrance.
APPLICATion periods
The November to January application period is open to candidates worldwide, offering the opportunity to apply for Erasmus+ EMJM scholarships or to join the programme on a self-funded basis.
The March to May application period is specifically for European candidates, who can apply on a non-scholarship basis on a rolling admission procedure. Rolling admission procedure means that we accept applications from March until the deadline or until all study places have been allocated.

academic background
Applicants are expected to hold a degree in a geospatial or closely related discipline.
The programme welcomes students from diverse academic backgrounds, reflecting the interdisciplinary nature of Earth Observation and Geoinformatics.
Typical fields of study
include (but are not limited to): Geography, Geoinformatics, Geology, Geosciences, Geospatial Information Science, Geomatics & Geodetic Engineering, (Spatial) Data Science, Cartography, Remote Sensing, Ecology & Conservation, Surveying & Mapping, Geodesy & Cadastre, Geophysical Engineering, Applied Physics, Agriculture, Resource Management, Meteorology & Climate Science, Urban and Regional Planning, Landscape and Spatial Planning, Public Administration, Infrastructure Management, Civil Engineering, Forestry & Environmental Sciences, Wildlife Conservation & Zoo Biology, Marine Science, Architecture & Town Planning, and Geomedia. Note: The list is illustrative rather than exhaustive, meaning candidates from related disciplines are encouraged to apply. However, supporting evidence on EO*GI experience, including academic transcripts, certificates, or other relevant documentation, must be submitted with the application.
Apply for admission, best-ranked students will win a fully funded Erasmus Mundus scholarship or will be nominated for a participation cost fee waiver.
The Selection Committee values – inter alia – documented Earth Observation & Geographic Information knowledge, working experience in the geospatial field, programming & software development experience. Student selection is not based on a single criterion, we evaluate various aspects, the overall portfolio, of each applicant. Vít Voženílek, Selection Committee Chair
You are welcome to apply if you are from Europe or an international candidate with a geospatial technology background.
Submit an outstanding application, the Erasmus+ funded programme offers well-funded scholarships of €1,400 per month and fee waivers to the – best-ranked – students. Sébastien Lefèvre, Selection Committee Chair
