Join the Copernicus Master in Digital Earth (CDE), a two-year, full-time Master of Science postgraduate programme that prepares the next generation of leaders to turn Copernicus data and Earth observation services, from remote sensing to in-situ data, into meaningful management and policy decisions within the evolving Digital Earth landscape.
Study in a diverse, global academic environment and gain the knowledge and skills to apply Earth Observation and Geoinformation (EO*GI) to pressing challenges such as climate change, environmental sustainability, and digital transformation.
Proudly co-funded by the European Union, the programme is fully accredited by the Agency for Quality Assurance and Accreditation Austria, and internationally recognized as Erasmus Mundus Joint Master (EMJM).
Study Copernicus & Digital Earth
With its strong focus on EO*GI, the European Copernicus programme, and distinctive specialization opportunities, the Geospatial Master of Science (MSc) in Geographic Information Science/Systems (GIS) delivers a truly one-of-a-kind learning experience. The programme is taught entirely in English and welcomes students from around the world.
In the first year, build a strong foundation in EO*GI, in the second year, shape your studies around your interests and career goals by specializing in GeoData Science & AI for Earth Observation or GeoVisualisation & Geocommunication.
