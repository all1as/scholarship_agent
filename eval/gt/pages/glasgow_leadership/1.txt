Global Leadership Scholarship
The University of Glasgow has several Global Leadership Scholarships available to International and EU fee students starting a postgraduate taught Masters programme in any discipline for Academic Session 2027/2028. The scholarship is awarded as a tuition fees discount for one year only.
To be eligible, applicants must
- demonstrate academic excellence and achieve grades equivalent to UK 1st Class Honours
- hold an offer of a place for a full-time 1 year Masters programme for September 2027
- have International or EU fee status
Please note that diplomas and certificates are not eligible for this scholarship. Only 1-year, full-time, in-person Master programmes are eligible, with the exception of Book and Paper Conservation MPhil and Textile Conservation MPhil where the discount will be applied for 1st year only.

How to apply
This scholarship will open shortly, please register your interest to receive a notification.
This scholarship is awarded on rolling basis so all applicants will receive a decision within 6 weeks from receiving their programme offer or submitting their scholarship application, whichever is later.
Closing date will be confirmed in due course, usually in late August.
University of Glasgow is committed to support as many self-funded students as possible which is why UofG discounts and scholarships cannot be combined with one another or any external partnership discounts (with the exception of Alumni Discount).
If you are offered more than one scholarship, the lower value award will be deleted from your account.
