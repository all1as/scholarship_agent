Science@Leuven Scholarship
Scholarship summary
The Science@Leuven Scholarship at the Faculty of Science of KU Leuven is a prestigious academic award established to recognise and support outstanding students who demonstrate a keen interest in scientific research and a commitment to the advancement of knowledge.
Motivated and talented international students interested in pursuing a Master’s degree at the Faculty of Science of KU Leuven are invited to apply for the scholarship. Scholarships are highly competitive, as the number of scholarships per academic year is limited. Only students with excellent results (comparable to at least Distinction at KU Leuven) in their previous curriculum will be considered.
Sponsor
This scholarship is financed by the Science@Leuven Fund

| Selection procedure | The selection procedure consists of several steps: Applicants apply for the Master's Programme of their choice. Only students admitted to a Master's Programme on this list will be considered for the scholarship. Applicants who have submitted a complete file and who meet all the necessary requirements are selected for the first round. The list of applicants per Master's programme is sent to the respective Programme Director, who ranks the students eligible for the Scholarship. Students are ranked mainly on the basis of their academic results. Other aspects, such as motivation and knowledge of the programme, may also play a role. The Selection Committee receives the list of selected applicants per programme and agrees on a ranking of all applicants. Students are ranked on the basis of their academic results, their knowledge of the English language and their motivation. The main selection criterion is the likelihood of achieving at least cum laude during their studies at KU Leuven The Selection Committee selects a number of students (usually around 8) for an online interview. The selected candidates will be interviewed online. Questions in the interview may include their motivation, their future plans, their extracurricular activities, their motivation to study at KU Leuven and the programme. The Selection Committee also takes into account how the applicant will act as an ambassador for the Science@Leuven Fund. The Selection Committee makes a final ranking of all the candidates who have been interviewed. The Board of the Science@Leuven Fund decides on the number of scholarships and awards them. |
