# KU Leuven Scholarship 2027 | Application Process | Fully Funded

Updated:

If you plan to study in Belgium with financial support, the **KU Leuven Scholarship**is one of the best opportunities for international students. This scholarship provides [fully funded](https://scholarshiproar.com/fully-funded-scholarships/) programs at Belgian universities across a wide range of fields. In this article, we will explain everything you need to know about the KU Leuven Scholarship, including its benefits, eligibility criteria, required documents, and a detailed step-by-step application process to help you apply successfully.

**KU Leuven offers a fully funded Science@Leuven scholarship for 2027-2028 for international students. This scholarship is available for [master’s](https://scholarshiproar.com/masters-scholarships/) studies and provides 12,000 euros to cover tuition fees, insurance, health insurance, and living expenses.**

The Faculty of Science and its alumni association, Science@Leuven, are proud to announce the Science@Leuven Scholarships. Motivated and talented international students interested in participating in an international master’s program of the Faculty of Science of the KU Leuven are invited to apply for a scholarship.

KU Leuven was founded in 1425 and has been a center of learning for almost six centuries. Today, it is Belgium’s highest-ranked university as well as one of the oldest and most renowned universities in Europe. KU Leuven offers high-quality academic education to its students at a wide level.

## Scholarship Coverage

The KU Leuven Scholarship provides the recipient with:

* The amount of the scholarship can be up to 12,000 euros for one year. The Board of the Science@Leuven Fund will decide exactly how much a scholarship will be. The scholarship will always cover the tuition fee for one year, insurance, and basic health insurance coverage. The amount awarded for living expenses can vary.
* If a student is awarded the full scholarship of 12,000 euros, it is still advisable to bring some extra money for unforeseen expenses, including books, supplies, and personal costs.
* Most master’s programs of the Faculty of Science are two-year programs. Only if the student achieves outstanding results in the first master’s year will the scholarship for the second master’s year continue.

## Eligibility Criteria for the KU Leuven Scholarship

To qualify for the KU Leuven Scholarship, the candidate must fulfill all of the following requirements:

* **Required Languages:**English (TOEFL minimum score of 94 or IELTS minimum score of 7).
* The applicants have not studied or worked at the University of Leuven before.
* The applicants have a bachelor’s degree from a foreign university that gives them access to the master’s program they are applying for.
* The applicants have not yet acquired a master’s degree or a PhD.
* The applicants can prove excellent study results during their former training, such as achieving high grades or receiving academic honors, which demonstrate their capability and readiness for advanced studies.
* The applicants show strong motivation to follow a master’s program at the Faculty of Science of the KU Leuven.
* The applicants are willing to act as ambassadors for the program.
* The students who have been awarded a scholarship will be asked to sign a contract that stipulates the conditions of acceptance.

## How to Apply for the KU Leuven Scholarship?

Please follow the steps below to avail this scholarship:

**(1)** Apply for the master’s program of your choice through the application tool of KU Leuven. Please read the [application instructions](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/application/instructions) carefully.

**(2)** Upload the following information via the KU Leuven application tool:

* Please provide a comprehensive list of course titles for which you have earned credit. Indicate the course size (in ECTS credits) and the result you obtained, preferably according to the ECTS scale; if a different scale is used, please provide a summary explanation of the meaning of the scores.
* Provide a short (about one-half to one page) description according to the standard guidelines for an ECTS study guide for the courses that you deem most relevant for the master’s that you are considering.
* Two reference letters from internationally renowned professors;
* Include a letter that expresses your motivation for the program.

**(3)** When you have completed and submitted your application for the master’s program, you will get a message on the screen that you have successfully applied.

IMPORTANT! Please capture a screenshot of this message. You will have to upload the screenshot when you register for the Science@Leuven Scholarship.

**(4)** Apply for the Science@Leuven Scholarship via this [link](https://wet.kuleuven.be/apps/fund/new.php).

**(5)** Your registration is complete after you have received an email stating that you have been registered for the Science@Leuven Scholarship.

**Important Notice**

* Only students who have complied with all the above requirements will be considered for the scholarship!
* You can apply for the scholarship immediately after you have applied for a certain program; you do not need to wait until you are accepted into the master’s program. However, your scholarship application will be considered only if you are accepted into the master’s program.
* You can find more details about the application process here.
