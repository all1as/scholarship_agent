SECAI Scholarships for Master and Diplom Students in AI
30 Jun 2026
1 Objective
The School of Embedded Composite AI (SECAI) offers scholarships to support M.Sc. students and advanced 
students in a German Diplom program whose studies are related to the topical focus of SECAI. The  
scholarships are intended to support particularly qualified applicants, and to a lesser extent also to  
promote internationalization and diversity of AI-related teaching programmes at TU Dresden and Leipzig  
University.
2 Eligibility
Candidates are eligible to apply to this programme if they will meet all of the following criteria for the  
whole duration of the scholarship:
 the content of their studies, chosen specialization, or topic of the project work planned under the 
scholarship is closely related to the research areas of SECAI;
 they are students in an M.Sc. program, or students in a Diplom program where they have  
successfully completed the first two years of Diplom studies (Grundstudium);
 they are students of TU Dresden or Leipzig University, or they are visiting a research group at TU  
Dresden or Leipzig University;
 they do not receive other scholarships or educational funding from public or private organizations.
It is possible to apply if some of the above criteria are not met (yet), but are expected to be met at the time 
when the scholarship would start. It is a prerequisite for receiving a scholarship that candidates continue 
to satisfy the above eligibility criteria.
3 Duration & Extent of Funding
The maximal duration for which a scholarship can initially be awarded is subject to the following upper  
limits:
 for scholarships related to concrete study projects (including the completion of a thesis): the  
regular time required for such a project according to study regulations;
 for scholarships granted to visiting students: the duration of the stay at TU Dresden or Leipzig  
University;
 for any other scholarships of students at TU Dresden or Leipzig University: the remaining duration 
of the recipient’s M.Sc. or Diplom studies according to the model study plan (“Regelstudienzeit”).
Shorter scholarship durations can be determined by the Selection Committee based on available budget  
and  the  specific  study  situation  and  progress  of  the  candidate.  If  the  prerequisites  for  receiving  a 
scholarship are no longer satisfied (see Eligibility), then the scholarship will end immediately.
The time of a scholarship can be extended upon request. The Selection Committee decides on such  
requests.
The monthly grant consists of a scholarship rate of EUR 934 for M.Sc. students. Any extra income must be 
declared, and the scholarship may be reduced by the amount of gross income exceeding EUR 603. For  
incomplete months, a daily rate of 31 EUR is applied. Short-term visits of at most 22 days by foreign  
students are funded at a daily rate of 42 EUR.
Foreign students who stay for more than six months may be entitled to a monthly family allowance as  
follows:
 276 EUR for their accompanying spouse who is not supported by other programs, private or  
public;
 219 EUR for the first and second accompanying child, 225 EUR for the third accompanying child,  
250 EUR for each further accompanying child, where only children are considered for which the  
applicant has no legal claim to public benefits (Kindergeld).
The daily allowance for incomplete months is 1/30th of the monthly allowance.
The scholarship does not include any other kind of funding. In particular, recipients still have to cover  
insurance costs and study fees.
4 Selection Procedure
A  Selection  Committee  consisting  of  SECAI  Fellows  reviews  and  decides  on  the  applications.  The 
scholarships are awarded in a competitive process, based on the reviewed application documents and an 
interview. The Selection Committee can reject applications without an interview. For ranking applications 
in specific calls, the Selection Committee may decide to use an internal scoring system for applicants.
Interviews are conducted by at least two reviewers appointed by the Selection Committee, of which at  
least one must be a member of that committee. Interviews can be personal meetings or video calls, where 
the latter should be preferred for remote candidates.
The  Selection  Committee  strives  to  ensure  that  the  selection  process  is  free  of  discrimination.  For 
example, applicants with disabilities or chronic diseases must not be disadvantaged. Other personal  
circumstances should also be taken into account, including, e.g., family care duties, past or current  
experiences as a refugee, and other special burdens. The selection in general, and interview dates and  
modalities in particular shall take such special situations into account. Applicants may therefore indicate  
such personal circumstances in their application on a voluntary basis.
Further aspects of the selection process will follow DAAD guidelines (“Merkblatt für die Gutachtertätigkeit 
in  den  DAAD-Auswahlkommissionen”,  07/2020).  In  particular,  the  selection  process  is  confidential; 
protocols of all selection meetings will be kept; conflicts of interests will be avoided. All applicants are  
eventually  notified  about  the  decision  of  the  Selection  Committee,  but  do  not  receive  detailed 
explanations for the decision. A fundamental legal right to claim funding within this program does not  
exist.
5 Selection Criteria
SECAI scholarships are primarily merit-based and will be reserved for excellent candidates. In addition, the 
selection committee will give due consideration to aspects of equal opportunities and life circumstances, 
on which applicants can provide information in their application. The selection criteria are:
Professional qualification
 University education, grades, and study time
 Professional fit for the intended work or study project
 Special academic achievements (awards, publications, past scholarships, etc.)
 Knowledge of the language of instruction or working language
 Ability to discuss professional topics during interview
 If applicable: relevant internships, work experience
Quality of the thesis project (if applicable)
 Quality of the thesis project, in particular taking SECAI topics into account
 Endorsement by prospective host or supervisor
Potential of the applicant
 Motivation: academic and personal reasons for the scholarship 
 Prospects: potential for further academic, professional, and personal development, especially in  
relation to SECAI
 Contribution:  degree  to  which  supporting  the  candidate  can  advance  SECAI  goals  (either 
professional or structural)
Extra-curricular qualifications
 Extracurricular knowledge and skills (suitably documented)
 Social, political, cultural, or family-related commitment
 Activities related to student life and university organization
Further selection criteria can be specified for concrete calls. For example, calls may be addressed to  
students of specific programs, to women in STEM-related subjects, or to international students.
6 Applications, Deadlines, Documents
Calls for scholarship applications will be published on the SECAI website and other public channels. Such  
calls may further restrict the eligible types of applications and set a specific deadline for applying.
Independent of such calls, current students of TU Dresden and Leipzig University, and visiting students  
who are endorsed by a host professor at TU Dresden or Leipzig University can apply at any time.
Applications should include all documents that are relevant to the selection criteria outlined above. This  
will always include a cover letter that clearly specifies what the candidate is applying for, motivates the  
application,  and  mentions  personal  circumstances  that  might  be  of  importance  to  the  evaluation. 
Moreover, a CV and official certificates about prior university education should always be provided.
Visiting students must further provide a written endorsement of the application by their host, and a  
description of the project work planned during the stay.
