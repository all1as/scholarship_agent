Finance and fees
You must pay the non-refundable application fee if you are a NON EU/EEA citizen, unless you hold a residence permit that exempts you from paying application fee. See the exemptions explained below.
Please note that EU/EEA citizens are not required to pay application fee.
Payment instructions
All application fees payments are made through our Webshop.
The application fee is 150 EUR (1150 DKK). One application fee covers all applications (max 3) to Aalborg University for the specific intake and cannot be transferred to another intake.
When you have completed the payment, you will receive a receipt to your email. Please upload the receipt to all your applications in the Application portal.
Students from outside EU/EEA countries are required to pay tuition fees in order to study at Aalborg University unless you hold one of the residence permits mentioned below.
Please note that students from EU/EEA countries are not required to pay tuition fees.
Payment instructions
Newly admitted students
Newly admitted student are required to pay tuition fee for the first two semesters. You will receive payment instructions in a separate letter right after your offer of admission. The payment deadline is 20 April for the September intake. Please note that this deadline cannot be extended.

Currently admitted students
When admitted to Aalborg University you will be charged a tuition fee prior to each semester.
The payment deadline is 1 month before semester start, thus 1 January for the spring semester and 1 August for the autumn semester.
Students from outside EU/EEA countries are required to pay tuition fees in order to study at Aalborg University (see rates below).
Please note that newly admitted students are required to pay tuition fee for the first two semesters at the same time in connection with admission.
Please note that students from EU/EEA countries are not required to pay tuition fees.
Aalborg University reserves the right to adjust the tuition fees if necessary. Tuition rates are subject to ministerial decisions, and we set and adjust them accordingly. If fees differ you will be required to pay the difference prior to your enrolment to Aalborg University.

You are exempt from paying application fee and tuition fee if you have one of the following residence permits:
- A permanent residence permit.
- A residence permit with the possibility of a permanent residency.
- A temporary residence permit with a view to temporary residence in Denmark after the Danish Aliens Act, Consolidation section 7 (1, 2 and 3), and section 8 (1 and 2).
- A temporary residence permit with a view to temporary residence permit or with the possibility of a permanent residency after the Danish Aliens Act, Consolidation section 9 (1), or section 9 c (1) as a result of a familiar association to a foreigner with a residence permit after section 7 (1 and 2) or section 8 (1 and 2)
- A residence permit after the Danish Aliens Act, Consolidation section 9 m (1) as an accompanying child of a foreigner, who hold a residence permit based on employment etc. after the Danish Aliens Act, Consolidation section 9 a.
- A residence permit after Danish Aliens Act, Consolidation section 9 b (1) (foreigners with humanitarian protection status)
- A temporary residence permit pursuant to Law no. 2055 of 16 November 2021 regarding persons who in Afghanistan have provided support to Danish authorities etc.
- A temporary residence permit in Denmark pursuant to Law no. 324 of 16 March 2022 regarding persons who have been displaced from Ukraine.
Students who have obtained EU status pursuant to the Executive Order on Residence in Denmark for Aliens Falling within the Rules of the European Union (the EU Residence Order) are not required to pay application fee.
Please note, you are also exempt from paying application fee if you hold a Danish upper secondary school exam, a Danish Bachelor’s degree (minimum 180 ECTS), a Danish Master's degree or one of the following Danish residence permits.
Application fee
The application fee is non-refundable for all reasons. Once you have submitted an application, you are liable for the application fee, even if you cancel the application. This means that if you pay the application fee, but change your mind about applying, you can only get a refund if you have not yet submitted the application.
Tuition fee
Before study start
If a student wishes to withdraw from Aalborg University prior to study start the paid tuition fee will be refunded in full.
After study start
If a student withdraws from Aalborg University within 20 September (for the September intake) or 20 February (for the February intake) 75 % of the paid tuition fee will be refunded. 
For non-EU students: If the students withdraws from Aalborg University within 20 September (for the September intake) or 20 February (for the February intake) due to not receiving a study and work permit or the application for a study and work permit being refused by SIRI, the tuition fee will be refunded in full. 
Later than 20 days after study start
If the student withdraws from Aalborg University later than 20 days after study start the student will not be eligible for a refund.
Who can get a scholarship?
Each September intake, Aalborg University grants a limited number of scholarships to individually selected applicants from non-EU/EEA countries based on strategic considerations and academic qualifications. Academic qualifications transcend national borders and are essentially global. The scholarships are financed by the Danish Government and awarded by Aalborg University.
All non-EU applicants are automatically considered when applying to a master’s programme. Therefore, it is not possible to apply for a scholarship separately.
Please note, Aalborg University only grants a limited number of scholarships - approximately 15 each year. We do not advise you to rely solely on receiving a scholarship when applying for a master’s degree at Aalborg University.
What is included in a scholarship?
A scholarship consists of a tuition fee waiver and a monthly stipend aimed at covering living expenses during the four semesters of your master's programme. 
The stipend rate for new students in 2026 is 11,225 DKK / 1503 EUR per month. Please note that you might be required to pay tax - read more here.
Be aware that the scholarship might not cover all your living expenses in Denmark. You may need to supplement your monthly stipend with your own savings, a student loan from your home country, or a student job. If you wish to learn more about living in Denmark (living costs etc.) you can read more here.
The stipend is paid for four semesters, which is the standard duration of a master’s degree. If your studies are prolonged, you will not receive further stipends beyond the first two years of study.
When will the scholarships be granted?
We will inform all applicants about the scholarship decision by the beginning of July at the latest, though it may be earlier. If you receive a scholarship, you will be informed about the details in a separate letter in the Application Portal.
We cannot guarantee that the scholarship decision will be made before the deadline for paying tuition fees. If you are required to pay a tuition fee and are subsequently granted a scholarship, your tuition fee will be refunded.
Please note, if you are granted a scholarship, you might not receive the stipend from the 1 September, as you must have a Danish CPR-number, a NemKonto, and be registered with the Danish tax authorities before we can pay the stipend. In that case, the stipend will be paid retroactively.
EU/EEA applicants
EU/EEA students cannot apply for scholarships at AAU.
You may be eligible for The Danish State Educational Grant and Loan Scheme (SU). You can read more here.
