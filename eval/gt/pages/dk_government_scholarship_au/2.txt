You can apply for all 50+ English-taught Master’s programmes in our Summer intake.
The application round opens 1 November.
You can apply for a limited number of programmes in our Winter intake.
Application opens in 24 August 2026.
EU applicants include applicants from the EU/EEA countries and Switzerland
Non-EU applicants include applicants from outside the EU/EEA countries and Switzerland
Note: If you are Non-EU citizens with a permanent residence permit (or equivalent) in Denmark you can also apply by the 1 March deadline for the Summer intake (or the 15 October deadline for the Winter Intake).
