Check Your Eligibility and Apply
If you have read about the Rhodes Scholarship and think that it is for you, the next step is to check your eligibility and identify which constituency you should apply through.
Enter your country in the box below and click ‘check your eligibility’ to learn more. If you possess dual citizenship, or have lived in a number of countries, we suggest that you choose the country you have the strongest connection to, for instance: the country of your citizenship, or the country you have lived in for the longest.
