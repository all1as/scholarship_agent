# University of Twente Scholarship 2027 | Step by Step

Updated:

The **University of Twente Scholarship** is one of the best opportunities available for excellent international students to study in the **Netherlands**. This [fully funded](https://scholarshiproar.com/fully-funded-scholarships/) scholarship is offered to outstanding non-EU/EEA students who are applying for a [master’s](https://scholarshiproar.com/masters-scholarships/) degree program at the University of Twente. The University of Twente Scholarship provides financial support ranging from €3,000 to €22,000 for one year to help students cover study-related costs. In this article, we have explained everything you need to know about the **University of Twente Scholarship 2027**, including its benefits, eligibility criteria, required documents, and a detailed step-by-step application process to help you apply successfully.

The University of Twente, also known as UT, is a well-known public research university located in Enschede, the Netherlands. The university is recognized for its strong focus on technology, innovation, entrepreneurship, engineering, social sciences, health sciences, and interdisciplinary education.

The University of Twente Scholarship, also called UTS, is designed for talented students from non-EU/EEA countries who want to join one of the qualifying UT master’s programs. This scholarship is highly competitive and is awarded to students who belong to the best 5 to 10 percent of their graduation class.

Applicants should note that the UTS is a separate procedure from admission to a master’s program. Students must first apply for a qualifying master’s program at the University of Twente and receive a conditional or provisional admission letter before they can apply for the scholarship.

These scholarships vary from government grants to funding by organizations or private persons, including options specifically designed for international students and those pursuing specific fields of study. In addition, faculties and the University of Twente Scholarship Foundation (UTS) offer a limited number of scholarships to excellent students. Two scholarships can be awarded to UTS recipients: [the Kipaji Scholarship](https://www.utwente.nl/en/education/scholarship-finder/kipaji-scholarship/) and [the Professor de Winter Scholarship](https://www.utwente.nl/en/education/scholarship-finder/professor-de-winter-scholarship/). These scholarships have additional requirements.

## The University of Twente Scholarship Application Deadline

The application deadline for the University of Twente Scholarship 2027 is **April 1, 2027 at 16:00 CET**.

The scholarship application form opens on **October 15, 2026 at 09:00 CET** for master’s programs starting in September 2027.

Applicants should apply for admission to a master’s program early because it may take up to 8 weeks to receive the result of the admission application.

## Scholarship Coverage

The University of Twente Scholarship provides the recipient with:

* € 3,000 – € 22,000 for one year.
* In the case of a two-year study program, students can receive an additional UTS in an amount equal to that in the first year to cover the expenses of the second year. However, the scholarship will only be continued during the second year when students meet the process requirements for the UTS.
* You must have obtained:
  + An average grade of 7 (out of 10) at the end of the third quarter of the first year
  + At least 50% of European Credits at the end of the third quarter of the first year

### [The Kipaji Scholarship](https://www.utwente.nl/en/education/scholarship-finder/kipaji-scholarship/)

University of Twente Scholarship recipients may also be eligible for an additional scholarship.

For programs starting in September 2027, UTS recipients can be considered for the **Kipaji Scholarship** if they meet its additional requirements.

Students who are interested in the Kipaji Scholarship should indicate their interest in their UTS application motivation.

### [The Professor de Winter Scholarship](https://www.utwente.nl/en/education/scholarship-finder/professor-de-winter-scholarship/)

This scholarship is only available to **excellent female students**who received a UTS scholarship for the same academic year.

* € 10,000 per year for the full duration of your Master’s programme.
* If you have applied for a two-year programme, make sure you read the information about two-year study programmes below.
* Have a look at the [overview of the estimated costs of living](https://www.utwente.nl/en/education/international-students/preparing/getting-ready/) in the Netherlands (excluding tuition fees).

## Eligibility Criteria for the University of Twente Scholarship

To qualify for the University of Twente Scholarship (UTS), you must fulfill the following requirements:

* Applicants must be from a non-EU/EEA country.
* Applicants must apply for a qualifying master’s program at the University of Twente.
* Applicants must receive a conditional or provisional admission letter for a UT master’s program starting in the academic year 2027/2028.
* Applicants must have a student number.
* Applicants must not have graduated from a University of Twente undergraduate or graduate program.
* Applicants must comply with the conditions for obtaining an entry visa in the Netherlands, if applicable.
* Applicants must meet the English language requirement.
* Applicants must not be eligible for a Dutch study loan.
* Applicants must belong to the best 5 to 10 percent of their graduation class.
* Applicants must be excellent students with strong academic records.
* Applicants must be able to cover the remaining study and living costs not covered by the scholarship.
* **English Language Requirements**
  + Applicants must meet the general English language test requirement of the University of Twente.
  + The accepted minimum English scores are:
    - **Academic IELTS:** 6.5 overall
    - **TOEFL iBT:** 90 overall
  + Applicants must also meet the speaking subscore requirement:
    - **IELTS Speaking:** 6.0
    - **TOEFL iBT Speaking:** 20

## Required Documents for University of Twente Scholarship

Applicants may be required to prepare the following documents:

* Online master’s admission application
* Conditional or provisional admission letter from the University of Twente
* Student number
* Academic transcripts
* Bachelor’s degree certificate or expected graduation certificate
* English language test result
* Curriculum Vitae or resume
* Motivation letter or scholarship motivation
* Passport copy
* Any additional documents required by the selected master’s program

Required documents may vary depending on the master’s program. Applicants should follow the instructions provided by the University of Twente admission system.

## How to Apply for the University of Twente Scholarship?

Please follow the instructions below to apply for the University of Twente Scholarship:

1. You have to apply for a master’s program first. You need to create an account on the university website [here](https://osiris.utwente.nl/inkomend/Welkom.do;OSIAANMELDINGSESSIONID=G1Rptl3WPj-ZHhrTIbI51D3TOxw6xQryHwHMso71b-b10FodzIPr!-1686182162).
2. Once you have applied for a Master’s program and received a (conditional) admission letter, you can apply for the scholarship with your student number.
3. [Click here](https://www.utwente.nl/en/education/scholarship-finder/application-forms/preapl-uts/) to complete the online application for the scholarship.
