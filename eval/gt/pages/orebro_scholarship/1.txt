Scholarships at Örebro University
At Örebro University we are working with a number of scholarship programmes for qualified students who are admitted to one of our international Master's Programme and are required to pay tuition fees.
Örebro University Scholarship
The scholarship programme is open for fee paying students having Örebro University as first priority, covering full tuition fees.
SI Pioneering Women in STEM
The scholarship programme is open to female citizens from eligible countries, covering both living costs and tuition fees.
SI Scholarship for Global Professionals
The Swedish Institute Scholarship for Global Professionals is open to students from eligible countries, covering both living costs and tuition fees.
In addition to the scholarships Örebro University is working with, there may be external scholarship and funding opportunities. Please note that these scholarships have different application deadline and requirements and are not managed by Örebro University. For further questions please contact the external organisation directly.
International Education Financial Aid Portal
The IEFA online database lists scholarships, grants, international student loan programmes and other information to promote study abroad.
Scholarshipportal
Scholarshipportal is a database presenting over 1,000 scholarships available in Europe and beyond. Select your desired level of study, the country you're from and where you want to study to see scholarships that match your profile.
Study in Sweden
StudyInSweden offers a list with country-specific scholarships offered by various organisations and foundations around the world.
European Funding Guide
Student from Europe can use the European Funding Guide to search for financial support for studies. European Funding Guide is a website compiling over 12,000 funding opportunities for students – that’s 80% of the 15,000 scholarships, grants, awards and loans on offer in the EU.
