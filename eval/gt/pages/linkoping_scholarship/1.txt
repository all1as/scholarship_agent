LiU International Scholarship
The LiU International Scholarship is aimed at supporting international master's degree students with significant academic potential who are required to pay tuition fees. The tuition fees for a student awarded a LiU International Scholarship are normally reduced by 50%. We offer a limited number of these scholarships to new students.
Important Update on Scholarship Eligibility 2026
Please note that new scholarship eligibility criteria have been introduced. Only applicants who meet the minimum academic standards outlined below will be considered for LiU scholarships. Applicants must have a GPA of 3.5 or higher on a 4.0 scale, a first or high second class degree, or an equivalent average to be eligible.
For example, this typically corresponds to 75% or above in India, 80–85% or above in China, or 8.0/10 or above in countries using a 10-point CGPA scale.
Institutional rankings will be considered as part of the evaluation process.
Applications which do not meet these minimum academic standards will not be considered.
Am I eligible to apply?
Students who have applied for a master’s programme at Linköping University and who meet all the following conditions are eligible to apply for a scholarship:
- you applied by the deadline for the first admission round
- you submitted all the required documents by the deadline
- you chose a master’s programme at Linköping University as your first priority (ranked as number 1 of 4)
- you have been admitted to your first choice in the First Notification of Selection Results (usually end of March)
- you are required to pay tuition fees.
