## About this position

EPFL is offering the **Master Excellence Fellowships** for students beginning a Master's degree at the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne in Switzerland.

The fellowship is aimed at outstanding candidates with a strong academic record and notable extracurricular achievements in areas such as research, entrepreneurship, leadership, sports, arts, or community service. It supports a wide range of EPFL Master's programs across engineering, computer science, artificial intelligence, data science, physics, chemistry, environmental sciences, biotechnology, robotics, materials science, architecture, energy systems, mathematics, and applied sciences.

**Funding:** CHF 10,000 per semester for up to 4 semesters, plus reserved accommodation subject to availability. The fellowship is integrated into the Master's admission process, and there is no separate fellowship application fee.

**Eligibility highlights:** applicants must apply to an eligible EPFL Master's program, have excellent academic results, and may only submit one Master's program application per year. Current EPFL Master's students and applicants already holding an EPFL Master's degree are not eligible.

**Application window:** the post lists two deadlines, 15 December for the first round and 31 March for the second round. The linked EPFL fellowship page should be checked for the exact year and program-specific instructions.

**How to apply:** apply through the EPFL Master's admission process, select the fellowship option on the application form, and submit the requested application file. Use the Master's programs portal and the EPFL Excellence Fellowships page for details.

## Funding details

Scholarship provides CHF 10,000 per semester for up to 4 semesters, plus reserved accommodation subject to availability. The fellowship is awarded alongside the Master's admission process and there is no separate fellowship application fee.

## What's required

Applicants must apply for an eligible EPFL Master's program, have an excellent academic record, and show strong extracurricular achievements such as research, entrepreneurship, leadership, sports, arts, or community service. Only one Master's program application is allowed per year, and students already holding or currently enrolled in an EPFL Master's program are not eligible. The fellowship is intended for outstanding students beginning their Master's studies.

## How to apply

Apply to an eligible EPFL Master's program and tick the fellowship box on the Master's application form. Submit the requested application file through the admission process. Check the fellowship eligibility page and program portal for details.
