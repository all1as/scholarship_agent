Two admissions rounds
There are two admissions rounds for international applicants, with different dates and deadlines. We recommend all international students to apply in the first (main) admissions round listed below.
This round takes place well before the semester starts and gives you time to pay tuition fees if required, apply for a residence permit, arrange housing, etc.

- Application deadline: 15 January 2027
- Supporting documents deadline: 1 February 2027
- Application fee deadline (if required): 1 February 2027
- Admission results published (Master's): 1 April 2027
- Admission results published (Bachelor's): 8 April 2027
Autumn semester starts: 30 August 2027

The second application round for EU/EEA/Swiss citizens explained:
The second admissions round in Sweden is usually:
- 15 March–15 April for courses & programmes starting in the autumn semester
- 15 September–15 October for courses & programmes starting in the spring semester
This round is mainly intended for EU/EEA and Swiss citizens, because they do not need a residence permit to study in Sweden. If you are a non‑EU/EEA citizen, applying in the second round will not give you enough time to receive a residence permit before the semester begins.
Why EU/EEA students are encouraged to apply in the first admissions round
Even if you are an EU/EEA citizen, we strongly recommend applying in the first admissions round as well:
- Some programmes only accept applications in the first round.
- You will receive your admission result much earlier, which makes it easier to plan your studies, housing, and travel.
Things to consider if you are applying in the second admissions round:
- Admissions results are published in July, or approximately seven weeks before the start of the autumn semester.
- You will have a limited amount of time to arrange with practical matters, such as findind housing, planning your travels, registering for your classes, getting insurance coverage, and much more.

Academic calendar at Umeå University
The academic calendar at Umeå University is divided into two semesters. The autumn semester normally begins in the beginning of September and ends in the middle of January. The spring semester begins in the middle of January and ends early in June.
