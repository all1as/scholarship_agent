Application procedure
Applicants are required to complete the online application form, providing their personal data and the following compulsory documents and forms. The application procedure consists of 3 steps:
- STEP 1 (Eligibility): Applicants will complete the Eligibility Check procedure by uploading the required documents (1-5) and submitting them before the deadline. Only applicants deemed to be eligible are allowed to carry on with the application procedure until its completion following either the EM Student Scholarship or the Self-Funded application pathways. Applicants deemed as non-eligible cannot apply again until the next call.
- STEP 2 (Application):
  - (A) Erasmus Mundus Scholarhip Application: Applicants deemed eligible to apply for EM scholarship at Step 1 will complete the Erasmus Mundus Student Scholarships Application procedure by uploading the required documents (6-12) and submitting them before the deadline.
  - (B) Self-Funded Student Application: Applicants deemed eligible as Self-Funded Student at any step will complete the Self-Funded Student Application procedure by uploading the required documents (1-11 & 13-15) and submitting them before deadline.
- STEP 3 (Enrolment):
  - (A) Erasmus Mundus Scholarhip Holders: Selected Scholarship holders will be individually informed to proceed with enrolment. They will also be requested to upload the required document (15)
  - (B) Self-Funded Students: Selected Self-Funded Student will complete the enrolment procedure by uploading the required documents (13-15) and submitting them before the deadline.
Mandatory documents for Elegibility Checking (Step 1)
(These must be submitted before eligibility deadline; upon clicking the STEP 1 submit buttom a submission confirmation will be sent by e-mail).
- Proof of Nationality: Passport or national identity card(1)
- Proof of Proficiency (PoP) in English (see Language Policy)
- Proof of Academic Achievement: Official Transcript of Records of the BSc Degree (pdf copy(1), (2); latest update including at least 4 semesters(3)). It is mandatory to submit the Spanish equivalent of the applicant’s Overall Grade Point Average (GPA) as well. Please follow the link: Equivalencia de la Nota Media de estudios realizados en centros extranjeros
- Curriculum Vitae: Mandatory Europass format (pdf)
- Eligibility form (online)
Mandatory documents for Complete Application (Step 2)
(These can be uploaded at any time before the given deadlines; upon clicking the STEP 2 submit button a submission confirmation will be sent by e-mail)
- BSc Degree Diploma (pdf copy(1),(2)): Bachelor degrees equivalent to 180 ECTS will only be accepted
- Motivation Letter: applicant’s personal statement to participate in the programme – (maximum length: 6000 characters)
- Two Reference Forms: submitted by academic/professional staff (on-line)(4)
- Proposed Study Plan Form: track of preference(5) (on-line)
- Photograph (passport type)
- Supplementary Information can be provided at the applicant’s convenience, uploaded as one single pdf or zip file
- Signed “Erasmus+ Scholarship Mandatory Declaration“ form, where applicants declare they comply with Erasmus+ eligibility requirements
Mandatory documents for Final Enrolment (Step 3)
- “Self-Funded Student Declaration“ form; only to be uploaded by Self-Funded Student applicants (signed PDF)
- Proof of Payment: uploaded to the online application system before the end of June
- BSc Degree Diploma: Unless e-Apostilled, the original Diploma (duly legalized) will have to be presented at the REM PLUS 2 Secretariat during the second mobility at the EHU for verification purposes
Application deadlines:
- Eligibility:21st November to January 31th (to be decided by the REM PLUS 2 JPB by February 15th)
- EM Student Scholarship applications: 16th February to March 15th (to be decided by the REM PLUS 2 JPB by April 15th)
- Self-Funded Student applications: May 15th (to be decided by the REM PLUS 2 JPB by May 30th). Conditional pre-acceptance letters supporting Self-Funded Student applicants to apply for other scholarships (e.g., National) and for visas can be only secured after April 15th for those who complete step 2B. A firm offer will be made only on completion of the general application procedure.
Notes about required documents:
        (1)A copy is enough to apply for admission. The Transcript may be incomplete at the time of application but must be complete by the time of final enrolment. The BSc degree may be uploaded after the submission deadline but always before definitive admittance and enrolment. Further on, originals or their certified copies will be required to proceed with final registration at the universities.
(2)Sworn translation into English is required to be eligible.
(3)The final academic transcript for the BSc degree must be submitted for final enrolment prior to the start of the programme in early September.
(4)Applicants must contact their referees via the online application system. Once the referees complete the Reference Form, the applicant will be notified.  In case a referee is not responsive, it will be the applicant’s responsibility to submit a new Reference Form. Ultimately, the applicant is solely responsible for ensuring that the two mandatory Reference Forms are submitted to the system before the deadline.
(5)At time of application and will be fully open to changes before definitive admittance.  At this stage of the application process, this information is only tentative. It will help to outline the applicant’s academic profile and might be open to changes before final admittance.
